About

I started as a Flutter developer — my BS Computer Science final year project, FallAlert, was a real-time safety system that detected phone falls using device sensors and automatically alerted emergency contacts. It was defended with 95% marks in my final viva.

Building FallAlert taught me something that now drives everything I do: the best systems are the ones that act the moment something needs attention — without a human having to notice first. That's the same principle behind good automation.

Today, as Founder of AL-KHAGGRI, I help businesses apply that same thinking to their operations — replacing manual, repetitive work with AI-driven systems that save time, cut costs, and scale without adding headcount.
